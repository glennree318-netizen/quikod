About Quikod

Quikod is a free QR code generator for links, WiFi logins, vCards, SMS, email and phone numbers. Every code is static: the data lives inside the code itself, so scans are never tracked, codes never expire, and everything works offline. No signup, no limits, no watermark.

Frequently asked questions

Static vs dynamic QR - what's the difference?

Dynamic codes (sold by most generator sites) route every scan through their servers - that's how they track you and why they charge subscriptions. Static codes encode data directly. Quikod only makes static codes: private, permanent, free.

Will my code stop working someday?

No. Static codes can't be deactivated - there's no server to turn off. A printed Quikod code works forever.

Why did my colored code fail to scan?

Scanners need strong dark-on-light contrast plus a quiet white border. Quikod warns you about low contrast and enforces the quiet zone - keep the defaults and you'll be fine.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Never. Encoding runs entirely in your browser, and the app works offline after first load.
